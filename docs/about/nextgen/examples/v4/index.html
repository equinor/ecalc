NextGen model example, In production (v4)

Energy network​

The energy network is shown at the top, and the energy demand from the process model (compressors and process pipelines) is shown in orange at the bottom.

This is an example of a NextGen eCalc model using YAML_VERSION: 4, which is in production. It is generated from the model builders in the eCalc library, and verified by the library tests. See the Process Reference and the Energy Reference for a description of every element, and the YAML Changelog for what changed between versions.

The model contains:

  • Four export trains with two compressors each, defined in COMPRESSORS, placed on a SHAFTS entry per train, and run in PROCESS_PIPELINES with a compressor model from DEFINITIONS.COMPRESSOR_MODELS.
  • A TIMELINE event that rebundles the compressors of the first export train.
  • One PROCESS_SIMULATIONS entry that distributes a common inlet stream between the export trains, with constraints for each train.
  • An ENERGY_NETWORK with fuel gas and electrical sources, generator, gas turbines, electrical cable and motors, drive trains for the export trains, an electrical consumer, and sampled compressors that are driven electrically, mechanically and by fuel.
  • MECHANICAL_EFFICIENCIES on export_train_1_drive, for both compressors on its shaft, and on export_train_2_el, for one compressor. The other compressor has efficiency 1.0.
